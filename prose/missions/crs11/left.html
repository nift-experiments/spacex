On Saturday, June 3, at 5:07 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 11th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-11) to the International Space Station from NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Approximately two days later on Monday, June 5, the spacecraft was captured at 9:52 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 12:07 p.m. ET.
