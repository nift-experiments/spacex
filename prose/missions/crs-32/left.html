On Monday, April 21 at 4:15 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 32nd Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-32) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station on Tuesday, April 22 at 8:40 a.m. ET.

After 32 days docked to the International Space Station, Dragon autonomously undocked from the space station at 12:05 p.m. ET on Friday, May 23. Dragon completed its 32nd Commercial Resupply Services mission to and from the International Space Station for NASA after successfully returning to Earth and splashing down off the coast of California on Saturday, May 24 at 10:44 p.m. PT.
