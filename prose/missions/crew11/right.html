The Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission previously flew the Demo-2, Crew-2, Ax-1, Crew-6, and Crew-8 missions to and from the space station. This was the third flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Ax-4 and a Starlink mission. Following stage separation, Falcon 9’s first stage landed on Landing Zone 1 (LZ-1) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

During their time on the orbiting laboratory, the crew conducted new research to prepare for human exploration beyond low-Earth orbit and to benefit humanity on Earth.
