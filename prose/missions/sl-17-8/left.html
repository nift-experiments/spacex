On Tuesday, September 2 at 8:51 p.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched 24 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4 East (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California.

This was the first flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster supporting this mission. After stage separation, the first stage landed on the Of Course I Still Love You droneship, which was stationed in the Pacific Ocean.
