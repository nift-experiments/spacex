On Wednesday, December 5 at 1:16 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 16th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-16) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately three days later on Saturday, December 8, 2018, the spacecraft was captured at 7:21 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 10:36 a.m. ET.

After 36 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 6:33 pm ET on January 13, 2019, and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California on Monday, January 14, 2019, at 9:10 am PT.
