On Saturday, November 26 at 2:20 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon's 26th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-26) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 7:39 a.m. ET on Sunday, November 27.

After 43 days, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Wednesday, January 11 at 5:19 a.m. ET.
