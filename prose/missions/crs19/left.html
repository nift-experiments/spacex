On Thursday, December 5 at 12:29 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s nineteenth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-19) from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida. Approximately two days later on Monday, December 8, the spacecraft was captured at 5:05 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 7:47 a.m. ET.

After 29 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 3:41 a.m. ET on Tuesday, January 7 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 7:42 a.m. PT.
