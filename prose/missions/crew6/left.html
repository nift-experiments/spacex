On Thursday, March 2 at 12:34 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s sixth operational human spaceflight mission Crew-6 to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the International Space Station approximately 25 hours later on Friday, March 3 at 1:40 a.m. ET.

After almost 186 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-6 astronauts returned to Earth and splashed down off the coast of Florida at 12:17 a.m. ET on Monday, September 4.
