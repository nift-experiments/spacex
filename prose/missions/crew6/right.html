This was the fourth flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew NASA’s Crew Demo-2, Crew-2, and Ax-1 to and from the space station. This was also the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission. Following stage separation, the first stage landed on the Just Read the Instructions droneship, which was stationed in the Atlantic Ocean.

During their time on the orbiting laboratory, the crew conducted over 200 science experiments and technology demonstrations in areas such as life and physical sciences to advanced materials, technology development, in-space production applications, and even student-led research.
