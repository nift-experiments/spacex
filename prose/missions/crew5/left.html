On Wednesday, October 5 at 12:00 p.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Dragon's fifth operational human spaceflight mission (Crew-5) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 5:01 p.m. ET on Thursday, October 6.

After 157 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-5 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Saturday, March 11 at 9:02 p.m. ET.
