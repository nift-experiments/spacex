On Sunday, July 5 at 6:50 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 29 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

Also on board this mission was Besxar’s Fabships, two pilot manufacturing pods. The Fabships were integrated onto the Falcon 9 first stage booster, kicking off a series of flight tests supporting Besxar's development efforts.
