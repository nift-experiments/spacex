On Sunday, February 19, at 9:39 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 10th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-10) to the International Space Station from NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Approximately four days later on Thursday, February 23, the spacecraft was captured at 5:44 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 8:12 a.m. ET.
