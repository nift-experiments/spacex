On Monday, July 18, at 12:45 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s ninth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-9) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Wednesday, July 20, the spacecraft was captured at 6:56 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 10:03 a.m. ET.
