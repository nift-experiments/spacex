On Wednesday, November 10 at 9:03 p.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Dragon's third operational human spaceflight mission (Crew-3) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station at 6:32 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 11.

After 176 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-3 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Friday, May 6 at 12:43 a.m. ET.
