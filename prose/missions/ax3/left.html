On Thursday, January 18 at 4:49 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Axiom Space's Axiom Mission 3 (Ax-3) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 5:42 a.m. ET on Saturday, January 20.

After 21 days in space, Dragon and the Ax-3 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Floridaon Friday, February 9 at 8:30 a.m. ET.
