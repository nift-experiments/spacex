On Monday April 2 at 4:30 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 14th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-14) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Wednesday, April 4, the spacecraft was captured at 6:40 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:00 a.m. ET.

After 30 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 9:23 a.m. ET on Saturday, May 5, and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 12:03 p.m. PT.
