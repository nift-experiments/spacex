On Sunday, September 21 at 1:52 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s fourth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-4) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Tuesday, September 23 the spacecraft was captured at 6:52 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:21 a.m. ET.
