SpaceX is targeting Thursday, October 15 for a Falcon 9 launch of the USSF-481 mission from Space Launch Complex 4E (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California. The four-hour launch window opens at 4:44 p.m. PT. If needed, there is a backup opportunity on Friday, October 16 at 4:35 p.m. PT.

A live webcast of the USSF-481 mission will begin about 10 minutes prior to liftoff, which you can watch here and on X @SpaceX.
