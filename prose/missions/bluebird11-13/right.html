This was the 30th flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Crew-5, GPS III Space Vehicle 06, Inmarsat I-6 F2, CRS-28, Intelsat G-37, NG-20, TD7, BlueBird 8-10, and 21 Starlink missions. After stage separation, the first stage landed on the A Shortfall of Gravitas droneship which was stationed in the Atlantic Ocean.
