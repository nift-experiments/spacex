On Wednesday, April 27 at 3:52 a.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Dragon's fourth operational human spaceflight mission (Crew-4) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon docked with the space station approximately 16 hours later at 7:37 p.m. ET.

After 170 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-4 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Friday, October 14 at 4:55 p.m. ET.
