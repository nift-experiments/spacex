On Saturday, August 26 at 3:27 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s seventh operational human spaceflight mission Crew-7 to the orbiting laboratory from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the International Space Station approximately 30 hours later at 9:16 a.m. ET on Sunday, August 27.

After 199 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-7 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida at 5:47 a.m. ET on Tuesday, March 12.
