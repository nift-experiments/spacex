On Saturday, January 10 at 4:47 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s fifth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-5) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Monday, January 12 the spacecraft was captured at 5:54 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 8:54 a.m. ET.
