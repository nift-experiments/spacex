On Saturday, March 2 at 2:49 a.m. ET, SpaceX launched Dragon’s first demonstration mission (Demo-1) from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 6:02 a.m. ET on March 3, becoming the first American spacecraft to autonomously dock with the orbiting laboratory.

After four days in space, Dragon returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Friday, March 8 at 8:45 a.m. ET.
