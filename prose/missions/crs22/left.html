On Thursday, June 3 at 1:29 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon's 22nd Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-22) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 5:09 a.m. ET on Saturday, June 5.

After 33 days, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Friday, July 9 at 11:30 p.m. ET.
