On Saturday, May 4 at 2:49 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 17th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-17) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Monday, May 6 the spacecraft was captured at 7:04 a.m. ET and then berthed to the station at 9:33 a.m. ET.

After 27 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 12:01 p.m. ET on Monday, June 3, 2019, and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California on the same day at 2:10 p.m. PT.
