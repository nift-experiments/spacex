On Sunday, December 6 at 11:17 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 21st Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-21) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station at 1:40 p.m. ET on Tuesday, December 7.

After 35 days, SpaceX’s Dragon undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, becoming the first cargo spacecraft to splash down off the coast of Florida on Wednesday, January 13 at 8:36 p.m. PT. This mission also marked the completion of the first autonomous undocking of a commercial cargo spacecraft from the International Docking Adaptor.
