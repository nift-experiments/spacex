On Saturday, May 30 at 3:22 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s second demonstration (Demo-2) mission from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida with NASA astronauts Bob Benken and Doug Hurley aboard the spacecraft. Approximately 19 hours later, Dragon autonomously docked with the International Space Station at 10:27 a.m. ET on Sunday, May 31. This test flight with NASA astronauts Bob Behnken and Doug Hurley on board the Dragon spacecraft returned human spaceflight to the United States.

Sixty-three days later, Dragon undocked and departed from the orbiting laboratory, before successfully splashing down off the coast of Pensacola, Florida on Sunday, August 2 at 2:48 p.m. ET.
