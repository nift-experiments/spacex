This was the fifth flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew Demo-2, Crew-2, Ax-1, and Crew-6 to and from the space station. This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission. Following stage separation, Falcon 9’s first stage landed on Landing Zone 1 (LZ-1) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

During their time on the orbiting laboratory, the crew conducted over 200 scientific experiments and technology demonstrations, including new research to prepare human exploration beyond low-Earth orbit and benefit humanity on Earth.
