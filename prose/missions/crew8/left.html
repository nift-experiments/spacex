On Sunday, March 3 at 10:53 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s eighth operational human spaceflight mission (Crew-8) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station approximately 27 hours later on Tuesday, March 5 at 2:28 a.m. ET.

After 235 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-8 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida at on Friday, October 25 at 3:29 a.m. ET.
