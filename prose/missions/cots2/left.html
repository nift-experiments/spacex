On Tuesday, May 22 at 3:44 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s second demonstration mission under NASA’s Commercial Orbital Transportation Services program (COTS 2+) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately three days later on Friday, May 25, the Dragon spacecraft was captured and then berthed to the space station at 12:02 a.m. ET.
