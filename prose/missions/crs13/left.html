On Friday, December 15, at 10:36 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 13th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-13) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Sunday, December 17 the spacecraft was captured at 5:57 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 8:26 a.m. ET.
