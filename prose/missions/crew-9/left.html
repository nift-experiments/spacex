On Saturday, September 28 at 1:17 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s ninth operational human spaceflight mission (Crew-9) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station approximately 28 hours later on Sunday, September 29 at 5:30 p.m. ET.

After 171 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-9 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Tuesday, March 18 at 5:57 p.m. ET.
