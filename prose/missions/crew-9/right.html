This was the fourth flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew Crew-4, Ax-2, and Ax-3 to and from the space station. This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched one Starlink mission. Following stage separation, Falcon 9’s first stage landed on Landing Zone 1 (LZ-1) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

During their time on the orbiting laboratory, the crew conducted over 200 scientific experiments and technology demonstrations to prepare for human exploration beyond low-Earth orbit and to benefit humanity on Earth.
