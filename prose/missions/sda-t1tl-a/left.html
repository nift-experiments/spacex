On Saturday, October 10 at 12:39 a.m. PT, SpaceX launched the Space Development Agency’s (SDA) fourth Tranche 1 data transport mission to low-Earth orbit (LEO) from Space Launch Complex 4 East (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California. This was the fourth of nine Tranche 1 missions Falcon 9 will launch on behalf of the SDA.

This was the sixth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched NROL-179, SDA-T1TL-E, and three Starlink missions. Following stage separation, the first stage landed on Landing Zone 4 (LZ-4) at Vandenberg Space Force Base.
