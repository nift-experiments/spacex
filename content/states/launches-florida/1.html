SPACE LAUNCH COMPLEX 40

CAPE CANAVERAL SPACE FORCE STATION

Cape Canaveral, Florida

The site's location on the southeast coast of the U.S. provides access to a wide range of low and medium inclination orbits frequently used by communications and Earth-observing satellites and by supply missions to the International Space Station. The site also allows access to geostationary orbits and Earth orbit for human spaceflight missions, as well as departures to the Moon and interplanetary destinations. National Security Space Launch and Starlink missions also liftoff from SLC-40.
