LAUNCH COMPLEX 39A

KENNEDY SPACE CENTER

Meritt Island, Florida

SpaceX is honored to launch from Kennedy Space Center's historic Launch Complex 39A, home of the Apollo and Space Shuttle programs. In addition to commercial satellite launches on Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy rockets, LC-39A also supports human spaceflight launches to Earth orbit, space station resupply, National Security Space Launch, and Starlink missions.
