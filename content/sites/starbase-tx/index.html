Environment

Protecting our coastal environment

Beach restoration

Cameron County beaches are eroding up to 20 feet per year. SpaceX is working with City of Starbase, Texas General Land Office, Cameron County, and other state and federal partners in rebuilding and strengthening beach dunes to help maintain the region’s natural defense against storms, flooding, and ongoing erosion.

Wildlife protection

Protecting wildlife and coastal habitats through careful planning, science-based mitigation, and partnerships with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and National Marine Fisheries Service. We review every project to avoid or minimize impacts to wildlife.

Environmental monitoring

We actively implement over 200 mitigation measures for Starbase operations, developed closely with federal and state agencies, including ongoing monitoring and scientific sampling of our local air, water, and coastal wetlands in accordance with all applicable regulations—while striving to exceed them.

City of Starbase

Incorporated in 2025, the City of Starbase is home to several hundred SpaceX employees and their families to support Starship development and operations.

Community

Supporting our neighbors

Scholarships

We are proud to help inspire the next generation of students in Cameron County. Our scholarship program empowers local students to pursue a college education and achieve their academic goals.

Beach cleanups

SpaceX organizes and hosts beach cleanups at Boca Chica Beach in collaboration with Cameron County, the City of Starbase, and the Texas General Land Office, helping to keep the shoreline clean and accessible for everyone to enjoy.

Local sponsorships

SpaceX sponsors local organizations and hosts community festivals, veteran events, and STEM programs that bring space exploration to South Texas.
