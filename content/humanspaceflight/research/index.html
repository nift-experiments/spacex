Current Research Opportunities

SpaceX is currently evaluating research proposals for Dragon human spaceflight missions on the following two categories:

Exploration-Focused Research

Crew-focused studies to understand the changes to humans in space or to assess tools that enhance health or well-being during long-duration missions. Specific areas of focus include expanding medical capabilities, efficient exercise and fitness solutions, and virtual or augmented reality as a multipurpose tool.

Scientific Payloads

Autonomous or crew-facilitated payloads designed for integration aboard future human spaceflight or cargo missions.
